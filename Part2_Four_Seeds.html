Part 1 §7: the notation does not travel in the poems. Hand any language model these three sentences and the card below before you ask it anything, or it will invent a framework of its own.

Read moments and poems in a four-seed notation. O: a thing made distinct. R+/R-: a bond,
cohering or repelling — structural, never good or bad. F: how much is changing, no direction.
V+/V-: how much it matters, and which way — the only home of good and bad.
Rules: no stakes before an object; direction rides the seed it belongs to, never F;
a seed that is absent is content — say so, do not fill it in. A lens, not a law.
  O            "this, not that"       a thing with edges      O^ forming · Ov fading · ∅ not yet
  R+ / R-      bonded to / kept from  structural, not moral   R^ a bond not yet closed
  F            how much is moving     intensity, no direction F=0 stillness · F[high]
  V+ / V-      draws me / pushes me   the only home of good/bad
                                      V+^ growth · V+v grief · V-^ dread · V-v recovery
  *V[high]     rings loud             *V->0  present but silent   (size is what the perceiver brings)
  P_x( )       a perceiver            P_x'  an altered perceiver
  A · B        both present           A -> B  becomes          P_a != P_b  two perceivers differ
  <seed>       a frame, named by the seed it conserves
  order        O -> R -> F -> V   a root (O beneath the rest), not a timeline
  floating     V<O longing · F<O chaos · R<O fusion · V<R urgency · F<R drift
  bound        R+·V+ love · R+·V- trapped · R-·V+ yearning · R-·V- revulsion
  in flux      F·V+^ growth · F·V+v grief · F·V-^ dread · F·V-v recovery · F·V± holding: flow / siege
  simple       O·V+ precious · O·V- taboo
  compound     R+·F·V+v haunt · R+·F·V-v reconciliation
  complete     O·R·F·V a whole moment

Appendix A of Part 1, verbatim. Measured once, with small models fine-tuned on this project's own writing: between zero and four of twenty-six framework questions right with no card; twenty-three of twenty-six with one.

Give it the card first. Then any of these. Watch for the failure the paper lists first: the model will want every poem to have all four seeds. It should not — absence is the notation's own content.
